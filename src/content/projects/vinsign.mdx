## Overview

Vinsign is a PKI-based digital signature system developed as part of Vindad’s legal-tech products—supporting secure signing workflows alongside the core legal services platform.

## Challenge

Digital signature systems sit at the intersection of security, reliability, and product usability. They need to perform under real use while meeting high expectations around trust and correctness.

## My role

As Senior Software Engineer at Vindad, I contributed to the development and maintenance of Vinsign with a focus on performance, security, reliability, and new feature development.

## What I built

Ongoing development and maintenance for Vinsign within Vindad’s legal-tech suite—improving and extending a PKI-based signing product alongside the core legal services platform.

## Technical approach

PKI-based digital signatures with attention to performance, security, and reliability. Work stayed at the product and systems level: features, maintenance, and operational quality for a security-critical workflow.

## Outcome

A maintained, evolving digital signature product supporting Vindad’s legal-tech offering—built with the same reliability bar as the rest of the platform.
