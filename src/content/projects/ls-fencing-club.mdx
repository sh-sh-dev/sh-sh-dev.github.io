## Overview

For LS Fencing Club, I designed and implemented a comprehensive athlete management system within a website platform—supporting club operations with a seamless user experience.

## Challenge

Sports organizations need clear tools for managing athletes and club workflows. The product had to be usable, maintainable, and deployable with confidence.

## My role

As Full Stack Developer (part-time, remote), I was responsible for designing and implementing the athlete management system within the website platform.

## What I built

An athlete management system integrated into the club’s website platform, covering the design and implementation needed for day-to-day club and athlete workflows.

## Technical approach

Laravel, Livewire, and Tailwind CSS for a cohesive full-stack experience, with CI/CD pipelines ensuring reliable and efficient deployments.

## Outcome

A working athlete management platform for LS Fencing Club—structured for usability and shipped with a deployment pipeline that supports ongoing delivery.
