## Overview

At Karazma, I worked on AI-powered learning systems that help users better understand educational content and act as virtual instructors by analyzing their projects and providing corrective feedback.

## Challenge

Educational platforms need more than static content. Learners benefit from feedback loops that understand their work and guide improvement—without turning the product into a generic chatbot.

## My role

As Senior Full Stack Engineer (part-time), I continuously enhanced the platform by designing and implementing a wide range of features, including these AI-powered learning systems.

## What I built

Platform features spanning product enhancement and AI learning experiences: systems that analyze learner projects and provide corrective feedback as virtual instructors, alongside broader feature development on the Karazma platform.

## Technical approach

Laravel and Livewire for full-stack delivery, with AI capabilities embedded into the learning product so feedback and instruction sit inside real educational workflows.

## Outcome

Richer learning experiences on the Karazma platform—AI used to support understanding and project feedback, delivered through ongoing product iteration.
