## Overview

Adad is a legal-tech SaaS platform focused on turning complex legal work into structured, reliable product workflows. It combines document automation, AI-assisted features, and production-grade backend systems into a product people actually use.

## Challenge

Legal work involves dense documents, domain-specific language, multi-step processes, and high expectations around reliability. The challenge is to build software that supports real operational workflows—not just demos—while keeping the product clear and usable.

## My role

As Tech Lead, I guide technical decisions, architecture, and delivery. I work across product engineering, backend systems, and AI features, connecting engineering work to product goals and helping turn ideas into production software.

## What I built

Work across the platform spans product architecture, APIs, workflow systems, and AI-powered features that support document-heavy processes. The focus is reliable delivery, iterative product improvement, and systems that scale with real usage.

## Technical approach

The platform is built with Laravel and PHP on a production backend, with AI capabilities layered into product workflows through retrieval-based approaches. Architecture decisions prioritize reliability, maintainability, and practical iteration based on user feedback.

## Outcome

Adad is my current focus and one important example of the work I care about: domain-complex problems, serious backend systems, and AI used where it creates real product value. It sits alongside prior work at Vindad, Karazma, Blueshell, and other products—not as the whole story.
