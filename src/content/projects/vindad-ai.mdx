## Overview

Vindad AI is an AI-powered legal agent grounded in local law, designed to analyze and process legal matters as part of Vindad’s broader legal-tech product suite.

## Challenge

Legal AI has to respect domain language, jurisdictional context, and reliability expectations that generic chatbots cannot meet. The work required systems that could support real legal analysis—not shallow demos.

## My role

As Senior Software Engineer at Vindad, I had full ownership of the architecture, design, and implementation of Vindad AI.

## What I built

End-to-end ownership of Vindad AI as a legal agent: architecture, design, and implementation, with the system grounded in local law for analyzing and processing legal matters. This sat alongside contributions to Vindad’s wider legal-tech products.

## Technical approach

Built with Python and retrieval-augmented approaches suited to domain-specific legal knowledge. The emphasis was practical AI for complex legal workflows—software design, RAG, and reliable processing of legal content.

## Outcome

A production-oriented legal AI system owned from architecture through implementation, contributing to Vindad’s legal-tech capabilities alongside the core platform and related products.
